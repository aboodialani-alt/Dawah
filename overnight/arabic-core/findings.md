# Library mismatches found while translating the core

The translators compared the library's English with the Arabic hadith texts in the open dataset. These are places where the library says something the source text does not, or the two disagree. They feed the core verification pass. Nothing in the library has been changed.

| Card | Library says | Source shows |
|---|---|---|
| 13 Eclipse (Bukhari 1043) | "they are two signs among the signs of Allah" | The dataset Arabic of 1043 does not contain that phrase; the library's own Arabic quote and the dataset end differently. Check the narration used. |
| 16 Jews and Christians recognised him (Tirmidhi 2733) | They "kissed his hands" | Dataset: kissed his hand and foot. Also graded differently by scholars (al-Albani: daif). The library gives two different reasons the Jews did not follow him (David's prayer; fear of their people). |
| 17 Revelation not epilepsy (Bukhari 1125) | Names the woman as Abu Lahab's wife | Dataset: "a woman of Quraysh". The identification comes from commentary, not the text. |
| 18 No personal gain (Ibn Majah 4153) | "Kings slept on silk" | Dataset: Kisra and Caesar among fruits and rivers. |
| 18 No personal gain (Bukhari 2916) | "Died in debt to a Jewish merchant" | Dataset: his armour was pawned for barley. Same event, different framing; use the hadith's wording. |
| 18 No personal gain | The Quraysh offer of wealth and power | No source given in the library. |
| 11 Romans | Tirmidhi 3193 "extend the term" detail | Graded daif by al-Albani; the dataset text is cut before that detail. |
| 12 Outside attestation | "Sinai's verdict above" | No Sinai quote appears earlier on the card. |
| 14 Literary challenge | al-Walid ibn al-Mughira report | No hadith number given; chain disputed. "The Legacy of Islam, p. 321" has no edition. |
| 19 Two channels (Bukhari 4986) | Zayd's account of collecting the Quran | Dataset excerpt cut before that part; Ali's words and Ibn Hajar's "two witnesses" have no source in the library. |
| 31 Human rights | Muslim 1218 for "your blood, your wealth" | The Muslim 1218 Arabic in the dataset is a different part of the narration; the phrase is in Bukhari 1739. Ahmad 23489 has no text or grading available. |
| 32 Religion and wars | "66 of 123 involving Islam" | The library attributes it to the encyclopedia in one place and to secondary tallies in another. |
| 33 Apostasy | Umar's words from the Muwatta | Translated from English; the Muwatta Arabic is needed before quoting. |
| 34 No compulsion | The apostasy verses speak of "loss in this world and the next" | Only 2:217 clearly says this. |
