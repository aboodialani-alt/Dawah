Debate dossier

Know their playbook before you sit down.

Paste what a specific person has said: transcripts, posts, comments. Claude finds the claims they keep returning to, matches each one to the library, and writes a briefing. It covers your answer, their likely next move, three questions to ask them, and the things you should not say because the library cannot back them yet.

Arguments that have not yet been through the core verification are marked as such in the briefing. Treat every answer as a starting point and check its sources before you rely on it live.
