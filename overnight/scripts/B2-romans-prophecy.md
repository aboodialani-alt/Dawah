## Objection check (what the sharpest critic says)

1. **"The text does not say when the clock starts."** True, and it is the weakest point. The script says it depends on the date of revelation. Do not claim a precise date. If you want to strengthen it, find a source for when Surah ar-Rum was revealed, and say it.
2. **"Which victory counts? The war ended in 627, not within nine years of 614."** Fair. The script gives the commentators' reading (first successes around 622 to 624) and names 627 openly. Do not say "Issus 622".
3. **"There is a variant reading, ghalabat instead of ghulibat, which flips the meaning."** The standard reading is ghulibat. The active variant is reported with weak chains according to the sources I found, and Tirmidhi 3193 (ghulibat wa ghalabat) is disputed in grading. Not raised in the reel. Keep an answer ready.
4. **"It was common knowledge in Arabia that the war was going on."** True, and the script does not claim otherwise. The claim is about the direction and the time limit: the losing side would turn it around.
5. **"Academics say the verses were edited after the event."** I could not confirm the library's description of Tesei's paper. Do not mention it on screen until you have read it.
6. **"Fulfilment by Muslims joining the story afterwards (Badr link)."** The Badr link rests on Tirmidhi 3192 and the commentators, so the script labels it as their reading.
