## Objection check (what the sharpest critic says)

1. **"You are using hadith to prove hadith."** The strongest reply, and the script has no answer to it yet. The reports above show what the early sources say, not that they are authentic, so they refute the claim only if you grant they are authentic. Fix: add one independent line before the fork, for example "The Sahifa of Hammam ibn Munabbih survives in manuscripts, and one version is in Ahmad's Musnad." That is supported by the sources I found. Scholars date Hammam's death to 101 AH (Hamidullah) or 130 AH (Jonathan Brown), and either date falls well before the "200 years" claim. **Decision for you:** add that line (about 6 seconds, then trim the Abu Shah beat), or keep the reel as an internal-consistency argument and say so.
2. **"The prohibition in Muslim 3004 was later and cancelled the permission."** The text does not give a date. Say only what the text says, as the script does. Do not claim an order.
3. **"Having a written note is not the same as a collected book."** True. The claim in the script is only that writing happened early. Do not stretch it to "collections existed" without the Hammam line and a source.
4. **"Abu Huraira's hadith are doubted."** Out of scope for this reel. It is a separate argument in Part V ("Abu Hurayra's large number of hadith").
