# Fact-check notes: Mongols and the fall of Baghdad

Status as of 7 October 2026. sunnah.com is blocked here (Cloudflare bot challenge, HTTP 403), so hadith texts were checked against the open hadith-api dataset (fawazahmed0/hadith-api, English and Arabic editions), which reproduces the sunnah.com texts, numbering and gradings. Still, a quick look on sunnah.com before posting is cheap.

| Claim | Status |
|---|---|
| Bukhari 2928: "fight the Turks, small eyes, red faces, flat noses, faces like shields coated with leather", and "a people whose shoes are made of hair" | **Verified** (Abu Huraira). Both sentences are in the same hadith. Arabic: صغار الأعين، حمر الوجوه، ذلف الأنوف، كأن وجوههم المجان المطرقة. |
| Bukhari 3590 (not used in the video) | Verified, but it names **Khuz and Kirman**, not the Turks. Do not cite it as the same narration. |
| Abu Dawud 4306: city "called al-Basrah" by the Tigris with a bridge; at the end of time the Banu Qantura come, "broad faces and small eyes" | **Verified** (Abu Bakrah). |
| Abu Dawud 4306 graded hasan | **Verified**: al-Albani Hasan; Muhyi al-Din Abdul Hamid Hasan; Zubair Ali Zai isnad hasan. |
| Baghdad founded 762 by al-Mansur | Confirmed in web sources. |
| Mongols under Hulagu took Baghdad in 1258, ending Abbasid rule there | Confirmed (siege 29 Jan to 10 Feb 1258). The Abbasid line continued only as a nominal caliphate in Cairo. |
| The Prophet died in 632 | Standard date. |

## Weak points to know before you post

1. **Abu Dawud names Basra, not Baghdad.** Identifying it with Baghdad is an interpretation. The script says so; do not cut that sentence. Note also that Basra itself was founded after 632 (about 636), so "a city not yet built" holds either way, but the video does not claim it.
2. **Bukhari 2928 says "the Turks".** The Mongol armies were Mongol-Turkic but not simply "Turks", and the Arabs knew the Turks well. The script states this objection openly (section 5). An earlier version said "a people" and hid the word "Turks"; that was fixed.
3. **Abu Dawud does not say the city falls to the Banu Qantura.** It says they alight on the river bank, the people split into three groups, and the third fights them. The video only says they "arrive".
4. The Ibn Taymiyya quote and the "report predates the city" claim (Said ibn Jumhan) are left out: still unverified.
5. Casualty figures are omitted on purpose; estimates vary widely.
