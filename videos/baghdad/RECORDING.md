# Recording sheet: The Mongols and the fall of Baghdad

About 60 seconds of speech. Record **one continuous take** on your phone, in a quiet room, mouth about a hand's width from the mic.

**Pause for 2 full seconds of silence between each numbered section.** Those pauses are how the video is timed to your voice. Do not pause inside a section.

Speak a little slower than normal, and say the last word of each section clearly before you pause.

| # | Say this |
|---|---|
| 1 | The Prophet, peace be upon him, passed away in the year 632. More than six hundred years later, a people arrived at Baghdad who fit his description. |
| 2 | In Sahih al-Bukhari, he describes a people with small eyes, red faces and flat noses, faces like shields covered in leather. In another narration, they wear shoes made of hair. |
| 3 | A report in Abu Dawud adds a great city on the Tigris, with a bridge. Some scholars understand it as Baghdad, which was founded in 762, over a century after he died. |
| 4 | In 1258, the Mongols under Hülegü took Baghdad and ended Abbasid rule there. |
| 5 | The objection is that the Arabs already knew the Turks. But the claim is not one detail. It is the whole picture: the appearance, the attack, and a city not yet built. |
| 6 | Weigh it as a whole, and check the sources yourself before you cite it. |

Pronunciation: Hülegü = hoo-LEH-goo. Abu Dawud = a-BOO da-WOOD. Bukhari = boo-KHAH-ree.

## Then

1. Save the take as `.wav`, `.m4a` or `.mp3` and put it in this folder.
2. Run: `python3 build_video.py --voice your_take.m4a`
3. The finished file is `baghdad_final.mp4` (1080x1920, ready to upload).

If the script says it found a different number of spoken sections than six, you paused too briefly or inside a section. Re-record, or tell me and I will adjust.

You can change any wording in `script.json`. Keep six sections.
