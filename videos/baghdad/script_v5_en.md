# Video script: The Mongols and the fall of Baghdad (v5, Ali Amery style, English)

About 190 words, roughly 80 to 100 seconds. Six sections. Pause 2 seconds between sections when recording. Quotes below were checked against the hadith dataset (see FACTCHECK.md).

**1.** Assalamu alaikum. They say this hadith is just a coincidence. Fine, let's read what it says. The Prophet, peace be upon him, died in 632. The Mongols entered Baghdad in 1258. More than six hundred years between them.

**2.** In Sahih al-Bukhari, number 2928: "You will fight the Turks: small eyes, red faces, flat noses, faces like shields covered in leather." And in the same hadith: "a people whose shoes are made of hair." What does it say? It describes the faces, and even the shoes.

**3.** Then a report in Sunan Abu Dawud, number 4306, about a city on the Tigris with a bridge, where the Banu Qantura arrive, broad faces and small eyes. The text calls the city Basra, not Baghdad. Some scholars understood it as Baghdad, and Baghdad was founded in 762, over a hundred years after his death. That is an interpretation, and I am telling you so plainly.

**4.** In 1258 Hulagu entered Baghdad, and Abbasid rule there came to an end.

**5.** They say: the Arabs already knew the Turks. True. That explains the faces. Does it explain the attack? Does it explain the city on the river? You are free: if each one is a coincidence on its own, fine. But how many coincidences do we need before we stop calling them coincidences?

**6.** I am not telling you this is conclusive proof. Read the hadith, read the history, then judge for yourself. Peace.
